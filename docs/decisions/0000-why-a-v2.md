# 0000. Why a V2, and what we take from the drafts and from V1

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11

## Context

V1 was a 3D shooter that grew step by step over some 30 steps: physics with Rapier, weapons, an ant that climbs, cars, a Möbius strip, networking with prediction, levels as files, mechanisms and logic signals, breakables, surfaces, simulated light, AI that uses the world, replays and three games on the same engine (about 20,800 lines and 169 tests). Several times we had to stop and redo foundations that had become too small (steps 10c, 14b, 16c, 16d, 16k).

Before starting V2, two drafts were analyzed:

- **An engine architecture proposal from scratch:** a detailed technical blueprint (layers, a fixed step in ten phases, a catalog of 20 capabilities, binary networking, a six-phase plan).
- **A strategic evaluation:** a review of the proposal that asks to shrink the scope, define determinism honestly and demonstrate before promising.

## Decision

**V2 is built from scratch**: new code and new tests. No code is copied from V1; **lessons** are brought over, most of them measured. The ones that become rules from day 0:

1. **All the state lives in the world** (ids, randomness, clock): in V1, the global id counter and the global randomness made two simulations steal numbers from each other.
2. **The simulation does not touch the screen**, and the compiler watches it (0003).
3. **The engine receives games as cartridges** and never imports them (mechanism and policy). The **Dojo** exists from the first day.
4. **Levels are data** and **gravity is a field**, not a constant.
5. **Measured determinism**: a hash of the state per step.
6. **An early networking skeleton** (phase 1), even if full networking comes later. The proposal put it in phase 5, against its own "no rework" rule. In V1, step 8's networking shaped everything.
7. **Measure before claiming**: every performance number with its scenario.

### From the architecture proposal

**Taken:** the three layers (a screenless core, presentation, games) and the server as a package; the Dojo as a consumer; the level as data; gravity as a field; an explicit order of the step's phases; the protocol to absorb new capabilities ("from narrative noun to mechanism", with admission requirements); cosmetic physics separated from the physics that decides; archetypes as data; the hooks to filter damage and translate keys; rotating platforms; navigation edges that require a capability; posture and material noise in stealth; swept hit volumes; the concrete performance scenario of step 1.1.

### From the strategic evaluation

**Taken:** a small, demonstrable core before many features; capabilities need a **composition protocol** (here: one exclusive locomotion mode plus contributions that add up, resolved in a single place); defining **which determinism** we promise (in the same program, yes; across browsers, it is not guaranteed: the JavaScript standard does not require `Math.sin` to give the same in all of them); separating **gravity, support surface and adhesion**; **sphere before cube**; a **visual physics lab** (vectors, forces, contacts); **reproducible experiments**; a **versioned world format**; Rapier behind a small boundary; and **demonstrations** as each phase's milestones.

**Postponed:** community and extensions, a visual capability editor, the research line (interesting, but it needs a bounded question).

**Disagreed:** that networking can wait until the end (see rule 6).

### What both were missing

The **web platform** (home page, the project, the tutorial, the Dojo and the games) and the **professional way of working** (git, continuous integration, tests in the browser): decisions 0001, 0002 and 0005.

## Consequences

- Starting from scratch is slower at first; in exchange, every piece is understood and ends up in the tutorial.
- V1's lessons are here, with their numbers, so they do not have to be discovered again.

## Alternatives discarded

- **Continuing with V1**: its structure carried the debts of growing without knowing where to.
- **Rewriting by porting V1 modules with their tests**: faster, but it skipped the learning and brought the old shape along. Discarded at the author's request.
